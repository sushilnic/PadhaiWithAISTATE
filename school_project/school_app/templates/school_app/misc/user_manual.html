Login & Getting Started

All Users

PadhaiWithAI has two separate login portals — one for staff (School, Block, District, State) and one for Students.

Staff Login (School / Block / District / State)

Open the login page

Go to the system URL. The login page opens by default.

Enter Email & Password

Type your assigned email address and password in the fields provided.

Complete the CAPTCHA

Solve the security CAPTCHA to confirm you are a real user. Click the refresh icon if the image is unclear.

Click "Login"

You will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard automatically.

Screenshot: Login Page
Login form with Email, Password, CAPTCHA fields and Login button

Student Login

Go to Student Login

Navigate to /student/login/ or click the "Student Login" link on the main login page.

Enter your Roll Number & Password

Use your school-assigned roll number and password. Default password is your roll number (change it after first login).

Select your Class & School

Choose your class from the dropdown to ensure correct data is shown.

Tip: If you forget your password, contact your school admin (for students) or district officer (for school/block users) to reset it.
Important: Do not share your password. Each login is tracked in the Activity Log.

Generating Practice Questions

Once you have solved the built-in NCERT questions with AI, you can create new practice questions in the same chapter's style. Click the Generate button on the Math Tools screen — the Question Options panel opens.

How to use:

  • Choose Question Type (Same, Multiple Choice, Fill in blanks, Short Answer Type).
  • Enter number of similar questions to generate (1–5).
  • Select Difficulty Level → Same Level, Harder, or Most Hard.
  • Choose Language (Hindi / English).
  • Click Generate Questions to create new AI-based practice questions.

Field reference:

FieldOptionsWhat it does
Question Type Same · Multiple Choice · Fill in blanks · Short Answer Chooses the format of the generated questions.
Number of Questions 1 – 5 How many new questions to create in one click.
Difficulty Level Same Level · Harder · Most Hard Controls how challenging the new questions are.
Language Hindi / English Language in which the AI writes the new questions.

Confirm the chapter

Check the blue Chapter chip at the top. Generated questions will belong to this chapter only.

Pick Question Type

Choose from the Question Type dropdown. If unsure, keep the default Same to match the chapter's style.

Enter number of questions

Type a number between 1 and 5 in the Number of Questions field.

Choose difficulty

Same Level for revision · Harder for stretch practice · Most Hard for advanced learners.

Pick language

Hindi for local-medium classes, English for English-medium.

Click Generate Questions

The AI takes 10–20 seconds. To abandon, click the grey Cancel button.

Solve or download

Each new question appears as a card. Click Solve for a step-by-step AI answer, or Download PDF to save a worksheet for your class.

A yellow How to use help panel stays visible below the form — it repeats these instructions on-screen so students or new teachers can self-serve.
⚠ AI-generated questions — teacher review recommended. Skim the generated set once before handing to students to catch any oddly-worded or ambiguous items.

District Officer (Collector) Guide

District Login

District officers have the most administrative power at the ground level. They manage schools, create tests, manage the academic calendar, and view district-wide analytics.

Creating a Test

Go to "Schedule New Test"

From the dashboard or navigation, click "Add Test".

Fill in Test Details

Enter Test Name, Subject, Max Marks, Test Date. Optionally upload Question Paper PDF and Answer Key PDF.

Save the Test

The test is created but NOT visible to schools yet.

Activate the Test

From the test list, click "Activate" to make the test visible and allow schools to enter marks.

Deactivate when done

Once all schools have entered marks, click "Deactivate" to close marks entry.

Only Active tests appear in schools' marks entry screen. Deactivated tests are still visible for reporting.
Uploading School Users (Bulk)

Go to Upload Users

Navigate to /upload-users/ from the dashboard.

Download the Sample Excel

Click the green "Download Sample Excel File" button. Open it and fill in your school data.

Fill Required Columns

The file needs: email, username, password, school_name, nic_code, block_id. Get Block IDs from the Manage Blocks page.

Upload the file

Choose your filled Excel file and click "Upload Users". School logins are created instantly.

Screenshot: Upload Users Page
Green download button at top, file upload form below, with Hindi and English instructions

Managing Academic Calendar

Click "Academic Calendar" → "Manage Events"

This page is only available to District Officers.

Add a New Event

Fill in the Event Title, Type (Teaching / Exam / Holiday / Meeting), Start Date, End Date, then click "Add".

View in Calendar Preview

The calendar preview updates immediately. All schools and block officers in your district will see the changes.

Delete an Event

Click the red 🗑 Delete button next to any event in the events list.

Activity Logs

Go to Activity Logs to see a full record of all user logins and actions in your district. Useful for security monitoring and accountability.

Security & Sessions

PadhaiWithAI protects your data with several layers of security. Here's what you should know as a user.

ProtectionWhat it doesApplies to
Session timeoutYou are logged out after 30 minutes of no activity. Log in again to continue.Everyone
One device at a timeLogging in from a new device automatically ends your other session — so a stolen password can't stay logged in.All admin roles
Account lockoutAfter 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes. Prevents password guessing.Admin logins
Password expiryAdmin passwords expire every 90 days. You'll be forced to change on next login.State / District / Block / School admins
Forced change on first loginDefault passwords (like 1234) must be changed before you can use any feature.Students & new admins
Math CAPTCHASimple math sums on login & password-change screens block automated bots.Login & password change
Encrypted transport (HTTPS)Everything you send/receive is encrypted end-to-end.Everyone
Rate limitingToo-fast repeated attempts on password reset / login endpoints get temporarily throttled.Everyone
Best Practices for Users
  • Never share your password with anyone — not even a helpful colleague.
  • Log out when you finish using a shared computer.
  • Use a password of at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.
  • If a screen ever prompts unexpectedly for your password again, close the tab and log in fresh — it may be a session expiring, not a real prompt.
  • Report anything suspicious (a login you didn't do, marks you didn't enter) to your District Admin immediately.
If your session ends mid-task (e.g., mid-way through entering marks), the page will bounce you back to login. Any unsaved data on that screen is lost. Save often on long forms.

Frequently Asked Questions

I forgot my password. What do I do?
Students should contact their school teacher. School admins should contact the Block or District Officer. District/Block officers should contact the State Admin to reset passwords.
I cannot see any test in the marks entry screen.
Tests must be activated by the District Officer before schools can enter marks. Contact your District Officer to activate the test.
The AI Math Solver is not giving an answer. What's wrong?
Check your internet connection. If the problem persists, try rephrasing the question. For image uploads, ensure the image is clear and well-lit. The AI may occasionally timeout — refresh and try again.
Why does the block dashboard show no charts?
Charts appear only when there is marks data entered for your block's schools. Ensure schools have submitted marks for at least one active test.
Can I upload students in bulk instead of one by one?
Yes. Go to Upload Student Data from the dashboard. Download the sample Excel file, fill in student details, and upload. All students are created at once.
The Academic Calendar is not showing. What should I do?
Events are added by the District Officer. If no events are added yet, the calendar will be empty. Contact your District Officer to add the academic schedule.
How many questions does AI Sathi answer per session?
There is no fixed limit. You can ask as many questions as needed in a session. Conversation history is maintained within the session.
Why can I see tests from other districts?
This should not happen in the current version. Each user's test list is filtered by their district. If you see tests from other districts, please report this to your admin.
The AI-generated question paper is missing a section or has empty questions. What happened?
The AI service occasionally returns malformed data for very long prompts. The system automatically retries that section on its own — usually fixing itself. If the issue persists, try generating a smaller paper (fewer questions per section) or split it into two papers.
Uploaded images (in Doubt Solver or OMR) are not loading — I see broken image icons.
This usually means the media files aren't being served. Ask your system admin to verify the media URL configuration. As a workaround, try re-uploading the image and reloading the page.
Why did my login get blocked with "Too many attempts"?
If 5 wrong passwords are entered from the same IP or account, the system locks it for 30 minutes to protect against automated attacks. Wait 30 minutes or ask your admin to reset it. This is normal security behavior, not a bug.
I was mid-way through entering marks and got kicked out. Why?
Sessions expire after 30 minutes of no activity. Save often on long forms. Also, logging in from another device automatically ends your session on the original device — check that no one else is using your account.
Are AI answers 100% correct?
No. AI-generated answers (Sathi, Doubt Solver, Question Paper, Math Solver) may occasionally contain errors, mis-simplifications, or ambiguous phrasing. Always cross-check with your textbook or teacher before using in exams. Every AI answer shows a warning banner reminding you of this.
Can parents see their child's marks?
Not directly — parents don't have accounts. But students can share the marks report from their portal (as printout or screenshot) with parents.
